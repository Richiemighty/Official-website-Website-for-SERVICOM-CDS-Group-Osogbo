Consumer Awareness Sensitisation at Osun Mall

Date: April 21, 2026
Venue: Osun Mall, Osogbo, Osun State

Introduction

Guided by SERVICOM's core values of Integrity, Professionalism, and Service, our team carried out a sensitisation exercise at Osun Mall to promote consumer awareness and fair treatment in everyday transactions.

Objectives

  • Educate consumers on their rights in physical and online marketplaces
  • Encourage citizens to speak up against unfair practices
  • Promote ethical business conduct among traders
  • Strengthen public understanding of redress mechanisms

Activities Carried Out

  • One-on-one engagement with shoppers on consumer rights
  • Interactive discussions with traders on fair pricing and quality
  • Collection of feedback on local market experiences

Impact

  • Many customers and traders gained practical knowledge
  • Traders committed to more transparent business practices
  • Strengthened trust through open dialogue
