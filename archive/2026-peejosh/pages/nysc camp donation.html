SERVICOM CDS Groups Donate Essential Items to NYSC Orientation Camp, Osun State

Date: June 10, 2026
Venue: Permanent Orientation Camp, Ede, Osun State

The SERVICOM Community Development Service (CDS) groups from Osogbo and Olorunda Local Government Areas have on Wednesday donated essential items to the NYSC Orientation Camp in Osun State as part of efforts to enhance the welfare of Prospective Corps Members undergoing the 21-day Orientation Exercise.

The courtesy visit and donation took place at the Permanent Orientation Camp, Ede, and was led by the President of SERVICOM CDS, Osogbo LGA, Popoola Joshua Olatayo, alongside his executive members, as well as the President of SERVICOM CDS, Olorunda LGA, Oladiti Racheal Omowunmi, and her executive team. The initiative was coordinated through their Schedule Officer, Mr. Kazeem Aderibigbe.

Led by

  • Popoola Joshua Olatayo — President, SERVICOM CDS Osogbo LGA, and executive members
  • Oladiti Racheal Omowunmi — President, SERVICOM CDS Olorunda LGA, and executive team
  • Mr. Kazeem Aderibigbe — Schedule Officer (coordinated the initiative)

Items donated

  • Sanitary pads
  • Tissue papers
  • Cartons of drinks
  • Biscuits
  • Face masks
  • Other welfare materials

Reception

The items were formally received by the State Coordinator, Mr. Ekeng Ita Kubiangha, who commended the gesture and described it as a timely and impactful intervention in support of Corps Members' welfare.

A commitment to service

The SERVICOM CDS leaders noted that the donation reflects their collective commitment to service, humanitarian support, and community development, particularly in improving the Camp experience for Corps Members at the start of their national service year.

They further expressed appreciation for the warm reception received from NYSC management and commended the Scheme's continuous efforts in maintaining a conducive environment for training, discipline, and integration of Corps Members.
