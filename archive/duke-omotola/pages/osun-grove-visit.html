Visit to Osun Sacred Grove

January 27, 2026 • Osun Sacred Grove, Osogbo, Osun State

On January 27, 2026, the SERVICOM CDS team of Osogbo Local Government Area embarked on a cultural and environmental awareness visit to the UNESCO World Heritage Site — the Osun Sacred Grove.

The outing aimed to deepen corps members' appreciation for Nigeria’s rich cultural heritage and promote environmental stewardship among youth serving in Osun State.

As custodians of public service values, the team reaffirmed its commitment to holistic community engagement — blending heritage preservation, ecological consciousness, and civic responsibility in line with SERVICOM’s mandate of grassroots impact.
