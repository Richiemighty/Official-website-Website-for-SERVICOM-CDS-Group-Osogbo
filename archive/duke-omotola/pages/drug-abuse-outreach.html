Drug Abuse Awareness & Stationery Donation

Date: March 10, 2026
Venue: Christ Africa Grammar School, Gbodofon, Aregbe, Osogbo, Osun State

Introduction

Our team, guided by SERVICOM's core values of Integrity, Professionalism, and Service, organized an outreach program to educate students on the dangers of drug abuse and empower them to make informed choices.

Objectives

  • Educate students on the risks and consequences of drug abuse, upholding the value of Integrity in making informed decisions
  • Equip students with strategies to resist peer pressure, demonstrating Professionalism in self-management
  • Promote a culture of wellness and healthy living, reflecting SERVICOM's commitment to Service to the community
  • Assess the school’s counselling support system through structured questionnaires

Activities Carried Out

  • Symposium on “Choose Life, Not Drugs: Your Future, Your Choice” by SERVICOM members
  • Welcoming address by the Schedule Officer
  • Interactive Q&A session promoting transparency and open dialogue
  • Distribution of 350 custom-branded pens and notebooks featuring SERVICOM CDS and NYSC Osogbo
  • Collection of student feedback via questionnaires to evaluate awareness levels and school support systems

Impact

  • 350 senior students (SS1–SS3) directly benefited from the program
  • Students corrected misconceptions about drug use and misuse
  • Several disclosed peer involvement in substance use — highlighting the urgent need for sustained intervention
