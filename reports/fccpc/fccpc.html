REPORT ON SERVICOM CDS SENSITIZATION

Collaboration between FCCPC and NYSC SERVICOM CDS Group

Event Details View Gallery

Event Details

Basic Information

  • Title: Sensitization Session for NYSC SERVICOM CDS, Osogbo L.G. by FCCPC Osun State Office.
  • CDS Group: Servicom
  • Local Govt.: Osogbo Local Government
  • Date: Tuesday, 16th of September, 2025
  • Time: 11:00 AM
  • Venue: FCCPC Osun State Office

Objective

To strengthen collaboration between FCCPC and NYSC Servicom CDS group, foster corps members' awareness of consumer protection laws, and sensitize participants on FCCPC's mandate in ensuring consumer rights and fair market practices.

Target Group

NYSC Servicom CDS corps members, FCCPC Osun State staff, and invited stakeholders.

Event Activities

Opening & Introductions

Opening prayer and self-introductions. Introduction of FCCPC staff.

FCCPC Presentation

Brief presentation on FCCPC history and mandate.

Department Presentations

Paper presentations of Consumer & Business Education, Quality Assurance & Development, and Surveillance & Investigation Departments.

Q&A Session

Interactive question-and-answer session with participants.

Gift Presentation

Presentation of gift by Servicom CDS group to FCCPC management.

Closing

Vote of thanks and closing prayer.

Sustainability & Impact

Continuous Partnership

The partnership fosters continuous sensitization on consumer rights and encourages corps members to become ambassadors of consumer protection.

Ambassador Program

Corps members are empowered to become ambassadors of consumer protection in their communities.

Future Collaboration

Sustains collaboration between NYSC and FCCPC for future programs and initiatives.
